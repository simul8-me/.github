We are a German company based in the Allgäu region, developing and distributing practical equipment for emergency medical services, paramedics, first responders, and rescue organizations.

Our philosophy is simple: **From real-world experience, for real-world use.**

Our products are developed by people with hands-on experience in emergency medical services. From initial concepts and prototypes in our own workshop to real-world testing and production, we focus on practical solutions that make everyday work easier.

### 🛠️ What we do

- **Emergency equipment** – Practical tools and accessories for EMS professionals.
- **Product development** – In-house prototyping, 3D printing, laser manufacturing, and small-batch production.
- **Custom solutions** – Specialized equipment for rescue services, organizations, and businesses.
- **Technology & automation** – Software, integrations, and digital workflows supporting our operations.

### 💻 Why we're on GitHub

Technology is an important part of how we work. We use GitHub to collaborate on technical projects, develop integrations, and share selected tools and solutions.

We believe in practical engineering, maintainable software, and learning from the developer community.
